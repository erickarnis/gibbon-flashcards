# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Eric, reviewing Gibbon's first two volumes in shuffled Anki sessions.

## Product Purpose

Recall the emperors, events, and narrative connections while enjoying Gibbon's prose. The existing CARD-GUIDELINES.md defines the learning task and content boundaries.

## Brand Personality

Clear, literary, restrained. Short direct answers and worthwhile quotations.

## Anti-references

Complete biographies on one card, generic reminders, and editorial instructions embedded in study text are excluded by the card guidelines. The layout should not add those distractions.

## Design Principles

- Ask one coherent question and lead with its answer.
- Preserve the distinction between required recall and optional historical colour.
- Give literary quotations readable space; exact wording is not required recall.
- Preserve image identity, qualifications, and provenance.
- Keep this work reviewable before Anki packaging.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No project-specific accessibility requirements are recorded. Design defaults for this preview are readable text, keyboard-operable disclosures, visible focus, uncropped images, and layouts that reflow on narrow screens. These are implementation choices, not a claim of certified conformance.
